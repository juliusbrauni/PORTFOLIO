About

I’m Julius, a photographer from Gunzenhausen in Bavaria. Since 2025 I’ve been photographing concerts, artists and the moments in between – the waiting before the show, the light that briefly sweeps across the crowd, the silence after the last song.

I’m not interested in the perfectly staged picture, but in the honest one. I work quietly, stay close and never in the way. My images should feel like a film you remember: atmospheric, warm, a little melancholic.

I’ve photographed shows such as Tanzen im Grünen and Paul Kalkbrenner. I shoot Fujifilm and edit with restraint, so a moment stays the way it was.
